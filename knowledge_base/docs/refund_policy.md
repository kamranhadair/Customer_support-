# Refund Policy

## Overview

We want every customer to be completely satisfied with our service. If you're not happy, we'll make it right.

## Eligibility

You are eligible for a full refund if:
- You request a refund within **30 days** of your purchase or renewal date.
- You have not exceeded **5,000 API calls** on the plan you are refunding.
- Your account is in good standing (no policy violations).

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds (prorated) are available when:
- You downgrade your plan mid-billing cycle. The credit is applied to your next invoice.
- You cancel a subscription before the renewal date. The remaining days are credited to your account.

## Non-Refundable Items

The following are **not eligible** for refunds:
- Add-on purchases (extra seats, storage upgrades)
- One-time setup fees
- Plans purchased at a promotional discount greater than 50%

## How to Request a Refund

1. Log in to your account dashboard at app.example.com.
2. Go to **Billing → Refund Request**.
3. Select the invoice and reason.
4. Submit the form. You will receive a confirmation email within 2 business hours.

## Processing Time

- Credit card refunds: **5–10 business days**
- Bank transfer refunds: **7–14 business days**

## Contact

For urgent refund requests, contact billing@example.com or open a support ticket marked **URGENT**.
