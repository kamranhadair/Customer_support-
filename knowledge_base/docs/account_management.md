# Account Management

## Updating Your Profile

1. Go to **Settings → Profile**
2. Update your name, email, or phone number
3. Click **Save Changes**

Note: Changing your email triggers a verification email to the new address.

---

## Password Management

### Changing Your Password
1. Go to **Settings → Security**
2. Click **Change Password**
3. Enter current password and then your new password (minimum 8 characters, 1 uppercase, 1 number)

### Forgotten Password
1. Click **Forgot Password** on the login page
2. Enter your registered email
3. Follow the reset link (expires in 1 hour)

---

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

### Enabling 2FA
1. Go to **Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication**
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy)
3. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm
4. Save your 10 backup codes in a secure location

### Disabling 2FA
1. Go to **Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication**
2. Enter your current 6-digit TOTP code to confirm
3. Click **Disable 2FA**

---

## Managing Team Members

### Inviting a New Member
1. Go to **Settings → Team**
2. Click **Invite Member**
3. Enter their email and select their role (Admin, Editor, Viewer)
4. They will receive an invitation email

### Removing a Member
1. Go to **Settings → Team**
2. Click the **...** menu next to the member
3. Select **Remove from Team**
4. Their access is revoked immediately

### Roles & Permissions
| Role | Can Edit | Can Invite | Can Billing | Can Delete Account |
|------|----------|------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Admin | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Editor | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Viewer | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |

---

## Deleting Your Account

> **Warning:** Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

1. Ensure you have exported any data you need
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**
3. Type your email address to confirm
4. Click **Delete Account Permanently**

All your data will be permanently deleted within **30 days** of the request.

If you have an active subscription, cancelling your account will also cancel the subscription without a refund for remaining days.

---

## Data Export

You can export all your account data:
1. Go to **Settings → Account → Export Data**
2. Select the data types (profile, tickets, reports)
3. Click **Request Export**
4. A download link will be emailed to you within 24 hours
